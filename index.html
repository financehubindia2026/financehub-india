Introduction: India's Market Fortitude

The Indian financial landscape continues to demonstrate remarkable resilience and dynamism even as global markets experience significant shifts. A recurring theme is the growing maturity of India's domestic investor base, particularly through Systematic Investment Plans (SIPs), proving to be a formidable bulwark against foreign capital outflows.

Simultaneously, the RBI is actively deepening market participation while globally, the tech sector grabs headlines with major index rebalances and blockbuster earnings from semiconductor giants.
