If there is one insurance product every earning individual should consider, it is term insurance. Term insurance is widely regarded as the simplest, most affordable, and most effective form of life insurance. It provides high financial protection for your family at a relatively low premium. Despite its importance, many people delay buying term insurance or purchase inadequate coverage. This guide explains term insurance, how it works, its benefits, and how much coverage you may need in 2026.

What Is Term Insurance?

Term insurance is a type of life insurance that provides financial protection for a specific period (term).

If the insured person dies during the policy term, the insurance company pays a death benefit to the nominee.

Simple Meaning

You pay a premium.

If something happens to you during the policy period, your family receives financial support.

How Term Insurance Works

Step 1: Purchase a Policy

Choose coverage amount and policy duration.

Step 2: Pay Premiums

Premiums can be paid:

  • Monthly
  • Quarterly
  • Yearly

Step 3: Coverage Begins

The insurer provides life coverage.

Step 4: Claim Settlement

If the insured passes away during the policy term, the nominee receives the sum assured.

Example of Term Insurance

Suppose:

  • Age = 30 years
  • Annual Income = ₹10 lakh
  • Term Insurance Coverage = ₹1 crore

Annual premium may be relatively affordable compared to the coverage amount.

If the policyholder dies during the policy term: Nominee receives ₹1 crore.

Why Term Insurance Is Important

1. Family Income Protection

Replaces lost income after the death of the earning member.

2. Debt Protection

Helps repay:

  • Home loans
  • Personal loans
  • Education loans

3. Children’s Future

Supports education and future goals.

4. Financial Independence for Family

Provides stability during difficult times.

Key Features of Term Insurance

High Coverage: Large protection at affordable premiums.

Fixed Policy Term: Coverage for a chosen duration.

Affordable Premiums

Lower cost compared to many traditional life insurance plans.

Flexible Riders: Additional protection options available.

Benefits of Term Insurance

Affordable Protection

Provides substantial coverage at a low cost.

Peace of Mind: Family remains financially secure.

Tax Benefits

Premiums may qualify for tax benefits under applicable laws.

Simple Structure: Easy to understand and manage.

How Much Term Insurance Coverage Do You Need?

A common guideline:

Coverage = 10 to 15 Times Annual Income

Example

Annual Income = ₹8 lakh

Suggested Coverage: ₹80 lakh to ₹1.2 crore

Human Life Value Approach

Coverage should also consider:

  • Future expenses
  • Existing loans
  • Children’s education
  • Family lifestyle
  • Inflation

Term Insurance Coverage Calculator Example

Annual Income: ₹12 lakh

Outstanding Home Loan: ₹25 lakh

Children’s Education Goal: ₹20 lakh

Emergency Requirement: ₹10 lakh

Recommended Coverage: ₹1.5 crore to ₹2 crore

Ideal Policy Term

Choose a term that covers your earning years.

Example

  • Current Age = 30
  • Retirement Age = 60
  • Recommended Policy Term = 30 years

Riders in Term Insurance: Riders provide extra protection.

1. Critical Illness Rider

Pays a lump sum on diagnosis of specified illnesses.

2. Accidental Death Rider

Additional payout for accidental death.

3. Disability Rider

Provides support in case of disability.

4. Waiver of Premium Rider

Future premiums may be waived under specific conditions.

Who Should Buy Term Insurance?

  • Salaried employees
  • Self-employed professionals
  • Business owners
  • Parents
  • Individuals with loans

Who May Not Need High Coverage?

Individuals with:

  • No dependents
  • Significant passive income
  • Large existing assets
  • Even then, basic coverage may still be useful.

Common Mistakes While Buying Term Insurance

Buying Too Little Coverage: Underinsurance is a common problem.

Delaying Purchase: Premiums increase with age.

Hiding Medical Information: Can lead to claim rejection.

Choosing Based Only on Price: Coverage quality matters more.

Ignoring Inflation: Future expenses may be much higher.

Factors Affecting Premiums

Age: Younger applicants usually pay lower premiums.

Health Condition: Good health generally means lower premiums.

Lifestyle

Smoking and other risk factors may increase costs.

Coverage Amount: Higher coverage means higher premiums.

Policy Duration: Longer terms may affect premium pricing.

When Should You Buy Term Insurance?

The best time is:

As soon as you start earning

Benefits include:

  • Lower premiums
  • Better health eligibility
  • Longer protection period
