A Demat Account

Without it, you cannot buy or hold shares electronically.

Today, opening a Demat account is quick, fully digital, and can be done within minutes.

This guide explains what a Demat account is, how it works, and how to open one step-by-step in India in 2026.

What Is a Demat Account?

A Demat account (short for Dematerialized Account) is an electronic account that stores your financial securities.

These include:

  • Shares
  • Mutual funds (in some cases)
  • Bonds
  • ETFs
  • Government securities
  • Instead of physical share certificates, everything is stored digitally.

Why Demat Account Is Important

Earlier, investors used paper share certificates.

Problems included:

  • Risk of theft ✗ Damage or loss ✗ Fraud ✗ Slow transfer process

Now with Demat accounts:

  • Safe storage ✔ Fast transactions ✔ Easy access ✔ Fully digital investing

How Demat Account Works

A Demat account works like a digital locker for your investments.

Step-by-Step Process

Step 1: You buy shares

Through a trading account.

Step 2: Shares are credited

They are stored in your Demat account.

Step 3: You hold or sell

You can keep or sell shares anytime.

Step 4: Selling process

Shares are debited from Demat account and sold in the market.

Demat Account vs Trading Account

FeatureDemat AccountTrading Account
PurposeStores sharesBuys & sells shares
FunctionHoldingTransaction
Required For InvestingYesYes

Both accounts are needed together.

Who Provides Demat Accounts in India?

Demat accounts are offered by:

  • Stock brokers
  • Banks
  • Financial platforms

Popular Platforms

  • Zerodha
  • Groww
  • Upstox
  • Angel One

Documents Required

To open a Demat account, you need:

  • PAN Card
  • Aadhaar Card
  • Bank account details
  • Mobile number
  • Email ID

Step-by-Step: How to Open Demat Account

Step 1: Choose Broker

Select a trusted stockbroker based on:

  • Charges ✔ Platform usability ✔ Customer support

Step 2: Fill Online Form

Enter personal details:

  • Name
  • PAN
  • Address
  • Bank details

Step 3: Complete KYC

Upload documents and verify identity.

Step 4: E-Sign Verification

Use Aadhaar OTP for digital signature.

Step 5: Account Activation

Once approved, Demat account is activated.

Time Required

Usually: ⏱ 10 minutes to 24 hours

Charges of Demat Account

Common charges include:

  • Account opening fee (sometimes free)
  • Annual maintenance charges
  • Brokerage fees per trade

Benefits of Demat Account

1. Safe Storage

No risk of losing physical certificates.

2. Easy Access

View investments anytime online.

3. Fast Transactions

Instant buying and selling of shares.

4. Paperless System

Fully digital investing experience.

5. Multi-Asset Holding

Can hold:

  • Stocks
  • ETFs
  • Bonds

Risks or Limitations

1. Market Risk

Investments can go up or down.

2. Charges

Some brokers charge maintenance fees.

3. Internet Dependency

Requires online access for trading.

Types of Demat Accounts

1. Regular Demat Account

For Indian residents.

2. Repatriable Demat Account

For NRIs who want to transfer money abroad.

3. Non-Repatriable Demat Account

For NRIs investing in India only.

Example of Demat Account Usage

You buy: 10 shares of a company at ₹100

Total investment:

  • ₹1,000
  • Shares appear in Demat account instantly after settlement.

Common Mistakes Beginners Make

1. Opening Account Without Research

Choosing wrong broker may increase costs.

2. Ignoring Charges

Annual fees and brokerage matter.

3. Not Understanding Trading vs Demat

Both are required together.

4. Overtrading

Frequent buying and selling increases costs.

Demat Account for Beginners

Best practices:

  • Start small ✔ Learn basics first ✔ Use trusted broker ✔ Focus on long-term investing
