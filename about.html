About FinanceHub India

📅 Updated July 2026 ✍️ FinanceHub India

FinanceHub India exists to make personal finance genuinely understandable for Indian readers — from a first fixed deposit to a first SIP to a first stock trade.

What We Do

We publish in-depth, plain-language guides covering mutual funds, the stock market, tax saving, banking, insurance, and everyday personal finance. Every guide is written to explain not just what a financial product or concept is, but how it actually applies to an Indian investor's situation — regulations, tax rules, and all.

Alongside our guides, the site offers live NIFTY, Sensex, Bank Nifty, and commodity data, a live financial news feed, and a set of calculators (SIP, EMI, FD, compound interest, goal planning, retirement, and tax) so readers can move from reading to doing without leaving the page.

Who Writes This

Our content is written and maintained by Financehub India, along with contributors researching and fact-checking each guide against current RBI, SEBI, and Income Tax Department rules. Articles are reviewed and updated as regulations, tax slabs, and market conventions change.

Our Standards

We are not a SEBI-registered investment advisor, and we never present ourselves as one. Everything on this site is for educational purposes only. We aim for accuracy, cite the reasoning behind every recommendation in plain terms, and update articles when facts change rather than leaving outdated numbers live.

Get in Touch

Have a correction, a topic request, or a partnership inquiry? Reach us through the contact form on our homepage.
