## What Is Share Market?

The share market is a platform where shares of publicly listed companies are bought and sold.

A share represents ownership in a company.

When you buy a share, you become a part-owner of that company.

### Example

If a company has 1,000 shares and you own 10: You own 1% of the company.

### Stock Market vs Share Market

Both terms are often used interchangeably.

<div class="table-wrap"><table class="data-table"><thead><tr><th>Term</th><th>Meaning</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Share Market</td><td>Buying and selling company shares</td></tr><tr><td>Stock Market</td><td>Broader market including shares, bonds, ETFs</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

In India, both refer to the same ecosystem.

## How Share Market Works in India

The Indian share market operates through regulated exchanges:

-   NSE
-   BSE

These exchanges provide a platform where buyers and sellers trade shares.

## Step-by-Step Process of Share Market

## Step 1: Company Goes Public (IPO)

A company issues shares to the public for the first time through an:

## IPO (Initial Public Offering)

## Step 2: Shares Get Listed

After IPO approval, shares are listed on:

-   NSE
-   BSE
-   Now they can be traded freely.

## Step 3: Investors Buy Shares

Investors buy shares through brokers using:

-   Demat account
-   Trading account

## Step 4: Price Movement Begins

Share prices change based on:

-   Demand and supply
-   Company performance
-   Market sentiment
-   Economic conditions

## Step 5: Investors Sell Shares

Investors sell shares to:

-   Book profit
-   Cut losses
-   Rebalance portfolio

## How Investors Make Money in Share Market

There are two main ways:

## 1\. Capital Appreciation

If stock price increases:

Example:

-   Buy at ₹100 Sell at ₹150
-   Profit = ₹50 per share

## 2\. Dividends

Some companies distribute profits to shareholders.

Example: ₹5 dividend per share annually

## Types of Share Market Participants

## 1\. Retail Investors

Individual investors like you and me.

## 2\. Institutional Investors

Examples:

-   Mutual funds
-   Insurance companies
-   Foreign investors

## 3\. Traders

Buy and sell frequently to profit from short-term movements.

## 4\. Long-Term Investors

Hold shares for years for wealth creation.

## Types of Markets in Share Market

**Primary Market:** Where companies issue new shares (IPO).

**Secondary Market:** Where investors trade existing shares.

## What Affects Share Prices?

## 1\. Company Performance

Revenue, profit, growth.

## 2\. Industry Trends

Sector performance impacts stocks.

## 3\. Economic Conditions

Inflation, interest rates, GDP growth.

## 4\. Global Events

Oil prices, wars, global markets.

## 5\. Investor Sentiment

Fear and greed influence prices.

## Example of Price Movement

If demand for a stock increases: Price goes up 📈

If selling pressure increases: Price goes down 📉

**Share Market Timing in India:** The market operates on working days.

**Trading Hours:** 9:15 AM to 3:30 PM (IST)
