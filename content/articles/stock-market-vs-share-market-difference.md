## What Is Share Market?

The share market refers specifically to the buying and selling of company shares.

A share represents:

### Ownership in a company

When you buy a share, you become a part-owner of that company.

### Example

If you own shares of a company:

<ul class="check-list good"><li><span class="ico"></span><span>You own a portion of that business ✔ You may earn dividends ✔ You may benefit from price appreciation</span></li></ul>

## What Is Stock Market?

The stock market is a broader financial system that includes:

-   Shares
-   Bonds
-   Exchange-Traded Funds (ETFs)
-   Derivatives
-   Other financial instruments

## Key Idea

## Share market is a part of stock market

## How They Work Together in India

In India, both terms are often used to refer to the same system involving:

-   NSE
-   BSE

These exchanges allow investors to:

<ul class="check-list good"><li><span class="ico"></span><span>Buy shares ✔ Sell shares ✔ Trade financial instruments</span></li></ul>

## Why People Get Confused

The confusion exists because:

## 1\. Common Usage

In India, people say “share market” in daily conversations.

## 2\. Financial Usage

Professionals often use “stock market” as the formal term.

## 3\. Overlapping Meaning

Both involve buying and selling ownership in companies.

## Example to Understand Difference

### Share Market Example

Buying shares of:

-   Reliance Industries
-   TCS
-   HDFC Bank

### Stock Market Example

Includes:

-   Shares
-   Mutual fund ETFs
-   Derivatives
-   Bonds

**Real-Life Analogy:** Think of it like this:

**Share Market = Fruits:** Only apples, oranges, bananas (shares)

### Stock Market = Supermarket

Fruits + vegetables + dairy + more (broader financial products)

## Functions of Stock Market

## 1\. Capital Formation

Helps companies raise money.

## 2\. Investment Platform

Helps investors grow wealth.

## 3\. Liquidity

Allows easy buying and selling.

## 4\. Price Discovery

Determines fair value of securities.

## Functions of Share Market

## 1\. Buying Shares

Investors purchase ownership in companies.

## 2\. Selling Shares

Investors exit investments when needed.

## 3\. Ownership Transfer

Shares change hands between buyers and sellers.

## Key Participants

## 1\. Retail Investors

Individual investors like beginners.

## 2\. Institutional Investors

Mutual funds, banks, insurance companies.

## 3\. Traders

Short-term buyers and sellers.

## Example of Market Activity

Company shares listed on: NSE

Investors buy and sell shares based on:

<ul class="check-list good"><li><span class="ico"></span><span>Demand ✔ Supply ✔ Company performance</span></li></ul>

## Common Misconceptions

## Myth 1: Stock Market and Share Market Are Different

Reality: Share market is part of stock market.

## Myth 2: Stock Market Is More Complex

Reality: It is broader but includes share market.

## Myth 3: Only Experts Use Stock Market Term

Reality: Both terms are widely used.
