## What Is Internet Banking?

Internet banking, also known as net banking or online banking, allows customers to access banking services through a web browser using a computer, laptop, tablet, or smartphone.

### Simple Definition

Internet banking enables customers to manage their bank accounts online through a secure banking website.

## Common Internet Banking Services

Customers can typically:

<ul class="check-list good"><li><span class="ico"></span><span>Check account balances</span></li><li><span class="ico"></span><span>View transaction history</span></li><li><span class="ico"></span><span>Transfer funds</span></li><li><span class="ico"></span><span>Pay utility bills</span></li><li><span class="ico"></span><span>Manage fixed deposits</span></li><li><span class="ico"></span><span>Request cheque books</span></li><li><span class="ico"></span><span>Download statements</span></li><li><span class="ico"></span><span>Manage beneficiaries</span></li></ul>

## How Internet Banking Works

### Step 1

Log in through the bank’s official website.

**Step 2:** Enter credentials and complete authentication.

### Step 3

-   Access banking services securely.
-   All transactions occur through encrypted banking systems.

## What Is Mobile Banking?

Mobile banking allows customers to access banking services using a smartphone application provided by the bank.

### Simple Definition

Mobile banking is banking through a dedicated banking app installed on a mobile device.

## Common Mobile Banking Services

Most banking apps offer:

<ul class="check-list good"><li><span class="ico"></span><span>Balance inquiries</span></li><li><span class="ico"></span><span>Fund transfers</span></li><li><span class="ico"></span><span>UPI payments</span></li><li><span class="ico"></span><span>Bill payments</span></li><li><span class="ico"></span><span>QR code payments</span></li><li><span class="ico"></span><span>Deposit management</span></li><li><span class="ico"></span><span>Loan information</span></li><li><span class="ico"></span><span>Investment access</span></li></ul>

## How Mobile Banking Works

**Step 1:** Download the bank’s official mobile application.

**Step 2:** Register the device and account.

**Step 3:** Log in securely.

**Step 4:** Perform banking activities from the smartphone.
