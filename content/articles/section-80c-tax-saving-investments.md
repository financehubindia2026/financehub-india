## Section 80C

It is one of the most widely used tax-saving provisions available to individual taxpayers.

Section 80C allows eligible taxpayers to claim deductions on specific investments and expenses, helping reduce taxable income and overall tax liability.

This guide explains Section 80C, eligible investments, benefits, and strategies for maximizing tax savings in 2026.

## What Is Section 80C?

Section 80C is a provision under the Income Tax Act that allows eligible taxpayers to claim deductions for certain investments and expenses.

### Simple Meaning

Money invested in approved financial products may reduce your taxable income.

## Why Section 80C Is Important

### Reduces Taxable Income

Helps lower the income on which tax is calculated.

**Encourages Saving:** Promotes long-term financial discipline.

### Supports Wealth Creation

Many eligible investments generate returns over time.

**Assists Retirement Planning:** Several options focus on retirement savings.

### Maximum Deduction Available

Under Section 80C, eligible deductions are capped at:

### ₹1.5 Lakh Per Financial Year

This limit applies collectively to eligible investments and expenses.

## How Section 80C Works

### Example

Annual Income: ₹10 lakh

Eligible 80C Investments: ₹1.5 lakh

Result:

Taxable income may reduce by ₹1.5 lakh under applicable rules.
