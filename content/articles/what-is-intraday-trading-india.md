## What Is Intraday Trading?

Intraday trading means:

## Buying and selling stocks on the same trading day

All positions are squared off before the market closes.

### Simple Meaning

You do not hold any shares overnight.

### Example

You buy 100 shares of a company at ₹200 in the morning.

Later in the day, you sell them at ₹210.

Profit = ₹10 per share

If price falls:

-   Buy at ₹200 Sell at ₹190
-   Loss = ₹10 per share

## How Intraday Trading Works in India

Intraday trading happens on exchanges like:

-   NSE
-   BSE

## Step-by-Step Process

## Step 1: Open Trading Account

You need:

-   Demat account
-   Trading account

## Step 2: Select Intraday Mode

Broker platforms offer an intraday option.

## Step 3: Choose Stock

Select liquid stocks (high volume trading stocks).

## Step 4: Buy or Sell

You can:

-   Buy first and sell later (long trade)
-   Sell first and buy later (short trade)

## Step 5: Square Off Position

All trades must be closed before market closes (3:30 PM IST).

## Key Features of Intraday Trading

## 1\. Same-Day Settlement

Positions are not carried overnight.

## 2\. Leverage

Brokers allow traders to trade larger amounts using margin.

## 3\. High Liquidity

Focus on actively traded stocks.

## 4\. Fast Decisions Required

Timing is extremely important.

### Intraday Trading Example

Capital: ₹10,000 Leverage: 5x

Total Exposure: ₹50,000

Stock Movement:

-   ₹100 → ₹105
-   Profit = ₹5 per share

## Strategies Used in Intraday Trading

## 1\. Scalping

Small profits multiple times a day.

## 2\. Momentum Trading

Following strong price movements.

## 3\. Breakout Trading

Entering when price breaks key levels.

## 4\. News-Based Trading

Trading based on market news.

## Risks of Intraday Trading

## 1\. High Volatility

Prices can move rapidly.

## 2\. Leverage Risk

Losses can also be amplified.

## 3\. Emotional Stress

Quick decisions can lead to mistakes.

## 4\. No Overnight Holding

You must exit even at a loss.

## Common Mistakes in Intraday Trading

## 1\. Overtrading

Too many trades increase losses.

## 2\. No Stop Loss

Can lead to large losses.

## 3\. Emotional Trading

Fear and greed dominate decisions.

## 4\. Ignoring Trends

Trading against market direction.

## 5\. Using Too Much Leverage

Increases risk significantly.

## Beginner Tips for Intraday Trading

## 1\. Start With Small Capital

Avoid large losses initially.

## 2\. Use Stop Loss

Protect your capital.

## 3\. Trade Liquid Stocks

Easier entry and exit.

## 4\. Avoid Overtrading

Quality over quantity.

## 5\. Learn Technical Analysis

Understand charts and trends.

### Example Intraday Trading Day

2:00 PM: Price drops to ₹152 Decision: Sell

Profit/Loss depends on timing and strategy.

### Taxation on Intraday Trading

Intraday trading profits are treated differently from long-term investments and may fall under trading income.

Tax treatment depends on:

-   Income classification rules
-   Holding period (same day = trading income)
-   Applicable tax slabs
