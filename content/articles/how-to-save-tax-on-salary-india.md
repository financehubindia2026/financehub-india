While taxes are unavoidable, there are many legal methods available to reduce your tax burden through proper financial planning. Many taxpayers miss opportunities because they wait until the end of the financial year or do not understand available deductions and benefits. The good news is that with the right strategy, you can lower your taxable income while simultaneously building wealth, protecting your family, and planning for retirement. This guide explains how to save tax on salary legally in India in 2026.</p>

## Step 2: Use Section 80C Investments

Section 80C remains one of the most popular tax-saving provisions.

Eligible investments may contribute toward the overall deduction limit available under applicable tax laws.

## Popular Section 80C Investments

**Employee Provident Fund (EPF):** Retirement-focused savings.

**Public Provident Fund (PPF):** Government-backed savings scheme.

**Equity Linked Savings Scheme (ELSS):** Tax-saving mutual fund.

**Life Insurance Premiums:** Eligible premiums may qualify.

**Home Loan Principal Repayment:** May qualify under applicable rules.
