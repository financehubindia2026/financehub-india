## What Is Banking?

Banking refers to the business of accepting deposits, safeguarding money, providing loans, and offering financial services to individuals, businesses, and governments.

### Simple Definition

A bank is a financial institution that manages money and provides financial services.

## Why Banking Is Important

Banks form the backbone of the financial system.

## Benefits of Banking

<ul class="check-list good"><li><span class="ico"></span><span>Safe storage of money</span></li><li><span class="ico"></span><span>Easy fund transfers</span></li><li><span class="ico"></span><span>Access to loans</span></li><li><span class="ico"></span><span>Support for investments</span></li><li><span class="ico"></span><span>Digital payment facilities</span></li><li><span class="ico"></span><span>Economic growth</span></li></ul>

Without banks, managing money would be far more difficult and risky.

## How the Indian Banking System Works

The Indian banking system operates through a structured hierarchy.

## Structure of the Banking System

### Level 1

-   Central Bank
-   ↓

### Level 2

-   Commercial and Specialized Banks
-   ↓

### Level 3

Customers and Businesses

The system is regulated to ensure stability, transparency, and consumer protection.

## Role of the Central Bank

India’s central bank is the: Reserve Bank of India

## Key Responsibilities

**Monetary Policy:** Managing inflation and economic stability.

**Currency Issuance:** Issuing and regulating Indian currency.

**Banking Regulation:** Supervising banks and financial institutions.

**Financial Stability:** Maintaining confidence in the banking system.

## Major Functions of Banks

Banks perform several important functions.

### Accepting Deposits

Banks accept money from customers and provide safety and accessibility.

## Common Deposit Types

**Savings Account:** For individuals to save money.

**Current Account:** Designed mainly for businesses.

**Fixed Deposit:** Money deposited for a specific period.

**Recurring Deposit:** Regular monthly deposits.

**Providing Loans:** Banks lend money to borrowers.

## Common Loan Types

**Home Loan:** For property purchase.

**Personal Loan:** For personal financial needs.

**Vehicle Loan:** For purchasing vehicles.

**Education Loan:** For educational expenses.

**Business Loan:** For business growth and operations.

**Payment and Transfer Services:** Banks facilitate money movement.

**UPI Payments:** Fast digital payments.
