## Dividend

Dividends are one of the most important ways investors earn passive income from the stock market.

This guide explains what dividends are, how they work in India, and why they matter for investors in 2026.

## What Is Dividend?

A dividend is:

## A portion of a company’s profit distributed to its shareholders

### Simple Meaning

If you own shares of a company, dividends are your share of its profits.

## How Dividend Works

## Step 1: Company Earns Profit

A company makes money through its business operations.

## Step 2: Board Decides Dividend

The company decides how much profit to distribute.

## Step 3: Dividend Announcement

Company announces:

-   Dividend per share
-   Record date
-   Payment date

## Step 4: Payment to Shareholders

Eligible shareholders receive money directly in their bank accounts.

## Example of Dividend

Company: ITC Limited

Dividend declared: ₹5 per share

If you own: 100 shares

Total dividend received: ₹500

## Types of Dividends

## 1\. Cash Dividend

Paid in cash directly to shareholders.

## 2\. Stock Dividend

Additional shares given instead of cash.

## 3\. Interim Dividend

Paid during the financial year.

## 4\. Final Dividend

Paid at the end of financial year.

## Why Companies Pay Dividends

## 1\. Share Profit

Reward shareholders for investing.

## 2\. Show Financial Strength

Consistent dividends indicate stability.

## 3\. Attract Investors

Dividend stocks attract long-term investors.

## 4\. Reduce Excess Cash

Companies distribute unused profits.

### Dividend Yield

Dividend yield shows how much return you get from dividends.

### Formula

Dividend Yield = (Annual Dividend / Stock Price) × 100

### Example

-   Dividend = ₹10 Stock Price = ₹200
-   Yield = 5%

## Advantages of Dividends

## 1\. Passive Income

Earn money without selling shares.

## 2\. Long-Term Wealth

Reinvested dividends compound over time.

## 3\. Stability Indicator

Shows strong financial health.

## 4\. Useful in Retirement Planning

Regular income source.

### Disadvantages

## 1\. Lower Growth Potential

Companies paying dividends may grow slower.

## 2\. Not Guaranteed

Dividends can be reduced or stopped.

## 3\. Taxation Impact

Dividends are taxable under income rules.

### Dividend Payment Process

## Step 1: Declaration Date

Company announces dividend.

## Step 2: Record Date

Only shareholders on this date are eligible.

## Step 3: Ex-Dividend Date

Stock trades without dividend benefit.

## Step 4: Payment Date

Dividend is credited to bank account.
