<p class="lead">Tax planning is not something that should be done only during the last few weeks of the financial year. Effective tax planning is a year-round process that helps individuals reduce tax liability, maximize deductions, improve financial discipline, and build long-term wealth. Many taxpayers lose valuable tax-saving opportunities simply because they fail to organize their finances properly. A structured tax planning checklist can help you stay prepared and make informed financial decisions throughout the year. This guide provides a complete tax planning checklist for Indian taxpayers in 2026.</p>
