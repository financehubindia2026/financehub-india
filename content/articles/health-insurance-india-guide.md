## What Is Health Insurance?

Health insurance is a contract between you and an insurance company where the insurer agrees to cover eligible medical expenses in exchange for a premium.

### Simple Meaning

You pay a small premium regularly.

The insurance company helps pay large medical bills when needed.

## How Health Insurance Works

## Step 1: Purchase a Policy

Choose a health insurance plan based on your needs.

## Step 2: Pay Premium

Premiums can be paid annually or in installments.

## Step 3: Coverage Starts

Medical expenses covered under the policy become eligible.

## Step 4: Hospitalization

If hospitalized, you can claim benefits.

## Step 5: Claim Settlement

The insurer pays covered expenses according to policy terms.

## Why Health Insurance Is Important

## 1\. Rising Medical Costs

Hospital expenses continue to increase every year.

## 2\. Financial Protection

Protects savings from unexpected healthcare expenses.

## 3\. Better Access to Healthcare

Allows treatment at quality hospitals.

## 4\. Family Security

Ensures loved ones receive medical care without financial stress.

## Types of Health Insurance Plans

## 1\. Individual Health Insurance

Covers one person.

### Suitable For

-   Young professionals
-   Single individuals

## 2\. Family Floater Plan

One policy covers the entire family.

### Suitable For

-   Married couples
-   Families with children

## 3\. Senior Citizen Health Insurance

Designed for older individuals.

**Suitable For:** People above 60 years

## 4\. Critical Illness Insurance

Provides lump-sum payout for specified illnesses.

**Suitable For:** Individuals seeking additional protection

## 5\. Group Health Insurance

Provided by employers to employees.

**Suitable For:** Salaried employees

## What Health Insurance Typically Covers

Coverage varies by insurer, but common benefits include:

**Hospitalization Expenses:** Room rent, nursing, and treatment costs.

**Pre-Hospitalization Expenses:** Medical expenses before hospitalization.

**Post-Hospitalization Expenses:** Follow-up treatment after discharge.

**Daycare Procedures:** Certain treatments not requiring 24-hour hospitalization.

**Ambulance Charges:** Emergency transportation costs.

## What Health Insurance May Not Cover

**Cosmetic Procedures:** Usually excluded unless medically necessary.

**Self-Inflicted Injuries:** Generally not covered.

**Waiting Period Conditions:** Certain illnesses may have waiting periods.

**Non-Medical Expenses:** Items not directly related to treatment.

## How Much Health Insurance Coverage Do You Need?

General guideline:

**Individual:** ₹5–10 lakh minimum coverage

### Family

₹10–25 lakh coverage depending on family size and city

### Metro Cities

Higher coverage is generally recommended due to rising treatment costs.

## Important Features to Check

**Sum Insured:** Maximum amount covered.

**Network Hospitals:** Hospitals offering cashless treatment.

**Waiting Period:** Time before certain conditions are covered.

**Claim Settlement Record:** Review insurer’s claim servicing history.

**Room Rent Limits:** Check whether room category restrictions apply.

## Benefits of Health Insurance

**Financial Protection:** Reduces medical expense burden.

### Tax Benefits

Premiums may qualify for tax deductions under applicable tax laws.

**Access to Better Treatment:** Enables quality healthcare.

**Peace of Mind:** Reduces financial stress during emergencies.

## Common Mistakes When Buying Health Insurance

**Relying Only on Employer Insurance:** Employer coverage may not be sufficient.

### Choosing Low Coverage

Medical inflation can make low coverage inadequate.

**Ignoring Waiting Periods:** Important to understand before purchasing.

### Buying Based Only on Premium

Cheapest plan may not provide adequate benefits.

**Delaying Purchase:** Health issues may affect eligibility later.
