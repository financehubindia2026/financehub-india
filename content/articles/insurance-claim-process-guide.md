## What Is an Insurance Claim?

An insurance claim is:

## A formal request made by a policyholder or nominee to receive benefits under an insurance policy.

### Simple Meaning

You ask the insurance company to compensate you for a covered loss.

## Why Understanding Claims Is Important

**Faster Settlement:** Knowing the process helps avoid delays.

**Better Documentation:** Correct paperwork improves approval chances.

**Reduced Stress:** Preparedness helps during emergencies.

### Financial Protection

Ensures you receive policy benefits when needed.

### General Insurance Claim Process

Although procedures differ by policy type, most claims follow these steps.

## Step 1: Inform the Insurance Company

Notify the insurer immediately after the incident.

Examples:

-   Hospitalization
-   Accident
-   Death of insured person
-   Theft
-   Property damage

## Step 2: Register the Claim

Provide:

-   Policy number
-   Incident details
-   Contact information
-   The insurer assigns a claim reference number.

## Step 3: Submit Required Documents

Documents vary depending on claim type.

## Step 4: Verification

The insurer reviews:

-   Policy validity
-   Coverage eligibility
-   Supporting documents

## Step 5: Claim Settlement

Approved claims are paid according to policy terms.

### Health Insurance Claim Process

Health insurance claims generally occur in two ways.

**Cashless Claim:** Available at network hospitals.

### Process

-   Show health insurance card.
-   Hospital contacts insurer.
-   Authorization is obtained.
-   Eligible expenses are settled directly.

### Reimbursement Claim

Used when treatment occurs outside network hospitals.

### Process

-   Pay hospital bill.
-   Submit claim documents.
-   Insurer reviews claim.
-   Eligible expenses are reimbursed.

### Health Insurance Documents Required

Common documents include:

-   Policy details
-   Hospital bills
-   Discharge summary
-   Doctor prescriptions
-   Diagnostic reports
-   Claim form

### Life Insurance Claim Process

Life insurance claims are generally filed by nominees.

## Step 1: Notify Insurer

Inform insurer about the policyholder’s death.

## Step 2: Submit Claim Form

Nominee completes required forms.

## Step 3: Submit Documents

Typically includes:

-   Death certificate
-   Policy documents
-   Identity proof
-   Bank account details

## Step 4: Verification

Insurer verifies policy and claim information.

## Step 5: Settlement

Death benefit is paid to the nominee.

### Motor Insurance Claim Process

Motor insurance claims are commonly filed after accidents.

## Step 1: Inform Insurer

Report incident immediately.

## Step 2: Record Evidence

Take photographs and collect details.

## Step 3: Vehicle Inspection

Surveyor may inspect damages.

## Step 4: Repair Authorization

Vehicle sent for repair.

## Step 5: Claim Settlement

Insurer settles claim according to policy conditions.

## Common Reasons for Claim Rejection

## 1\. Non-Disclosure of Information

Providing incomplete or incorrect details during policy purchase.

## 2\. Policy Lapse

Premiums not paid on time.

## 3\. Excluded Conditions

Claims falling under policy exclusions.

## 4\. Insufficient Documentation

Missing required documents.

## 5\. Fraudulent Claims

False information may result in rejection.

## Tips for Faster Claim Approval

**Inform Insurer Quickly:** Avoid delays in reporting.

**Maintain Documents:** Store policy and medical records safely.

**Understand Coverage:** Know what is covered and excluded.

**Provide Accurate Information:** Ensure consistency in documentation.

**Follow Claim Procedures:** Adhere to insurer requirements.

### Important Insurance Terms

### Claim Settlement Ratio

Percentage of claims settled by an insurer.

**Nominee:** Person designated to receive benefits.

**Sum Assured:** Maximum benefit payable under policy.

### Deductible

Amount paid by policyholder before insurance coverage applies.

**Exclusions:** Situations not covered by the policy.
