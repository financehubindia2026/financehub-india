Privacy Policy

📅 Updated July 2026 ✍️ FinanceHub India

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information FinanceHub India collects and how it is used.

Information We Collect

FinanceHub India is a static, publicly accessible finance guide website. We do not require account creation to read our content. We may automatically collect limited, non-personal technical information such as browser type, device type, approximate location (city/country level), and pages visited, typically through standard analytics tools and cookies. If you contact us via our contact form, we collect your name, email address, and message content. This information is transmitted through EmailJS, a third-party email delivery service, solely to respond to your inquiry. We do not use this data for marketing.

How We Use Cookies

Cookies help us remember your theme preference (dark/light mode), understand which guides are most useful, and improve site performance. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time; the site will continue to function, though some preferences may not be remembered.

Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services such as web analytics or hosting providers (for example, GitHub Pages or Vercel) to serve this website. These providers may collect standard server log information as part of delivering the site to you. We do not sell or share personal information with advertisers.

Data Security

Since FinanceHub India does not collect sensitive personal data such as financial account details, passwords, or payment information, the risk of data exposure through this site is minimal. Any contact information you voluntarily share with us (for example, via email) is used only to respond to your query.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at individuals under the age of 18, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us through the contact section on our homepage.
